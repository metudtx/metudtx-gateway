DIGITALTR and the application opportunity

A guided application route through METU DTX for four services supporting the digital transformation of manufacturing industry.

DIGITALTR is the Technical Assistance for Support for the Digital Transformation of the Manufacturing Industry. Co-financed by the European Union and the Republic of Türkiye, the project aims to increase the adoption of digital technologies and strengthen the competitiveness and digital transformation capacity of manufacturing industry.

The four services under the programme are provided at no charge to applicants. Applications are assessed against eligibility, technical merit, available service capacity and portfolio balance. Submitting an application does not create an automatic entitlement to the requested services.

METU DTX can support companies throughout Türkiye, with a primary focus on the automotive and machinery sectors while retaining the capacity to serve other manufacturing fields. Depending on the service, activities may be delivered on site, remotely or through METU DTX facilities.

Who can apply?

The applicant must be a legally registered company operating in Türkiye.

Manufacturing companies

Manufacturing companies seeking to improve their digital maturity, plan technology investments, validate existing systems or deploy new digital solutions may apply.

Technology providers

Companies developing software, hardware, digital systems or technology solutions for industry may apply to test, validate or improve their solutions, or to develop a joint solution with a manufacturing company.

Priority sectors include machinery, automotive and suppliers, electrical and electronics, white goods, chemicals and plastics, textiles and apparel, and medical technologies. The call is not limited to these sectors; applications from other manufacturing fields may be considered according to technical fit and available capacity.

Four service modules

You may select one or more services that match your needs in the same application.

1

Digital Maturity Assessment

Measures your current digital maturity, identifies priority improvement areas and creates an actionable transformation roadmap.

2

Digital Transformation Consulting

Provides expert support for decisions on technology investment, cybersecurity, process efficiency, change management, sustainability and access to finance.

3

Testing and Validation of Digital Systems and Technologies

Enables you to test, validate and improve digital solutions in realistic industrial environments before investment.

4

Joint Technology Solution Development

Supports the co-design, prototyping, adaptation and testing of a digital solution for a defined industrial need.

If your company has completed a valid digital maturity assessment within the last year using DMA, SIRI or a DDX/METU DTX approach, you may provide its details and apply directly for advanced services. The application remains subject to eligibility and technical assessment.
