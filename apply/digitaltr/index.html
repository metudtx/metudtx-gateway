METU DTX digital transformation services

Four services supporting manufacturing businesses from assessment through implementation.

METU DTX helps businesses assess their digital maturity, identify investment and transformation priorities, test technologies before implementation and develop solutions tailored to specific needs.

Applications are assessed against need, technical fit, service capacity and expected impact. The scope, schedule, delivery model and terms of the work are clarified after the initial technical assessment.

METU DTX serves companies throughout Türkiye, primarily in the automotive and machinery sectors as well as across other manufacturing fields. It delivers activities on site, remotely or through METU DTX facilities according to the nature of each service.

Assessment recognised for investment incentives: The METU-DTX Digital Maturity Assessment is a recognised assessment model used in digital transformation investment-incentive processes under the Republic of Türkiye Ministry of Industry and Technology Digital Transformation Support Programme.

Who can apply?

These services are primarily intended for mid-sized manufacturing businesses operating in Türkiye.

Manufacturing companies

Manufacturing companies seeking to improve their digital maturity, plan technology investments, validate existing systems or deploy new digital solutions may apply.

Technology providers

Companies developing software, hardware, digital systems or technology solutions for industry may apply to test, validate or improve their solutions, or to develop a joint solution with a manufacturing company.

Priority sectors include machinery, automotive and suppliers, electrical and electronics, white goods, chemicals and plastics, textiles and apparel, and medical technologies. The services are not limited to these sectors; applications from other manufacturing fields may be considered according to technical fit and available capacity.

Four service modules

Apply for one or more METU DTX services that match your needs.

1

Digital Maturity Assessment

Measures your current digital maturity, identifies priority improvement areas and creates an actionable transformation roadmap.

2

Digital Transformation Consulting

Provides expert support for decisions on technology investment, cybersecurity, process efficiency, change management, sustainability and access to finance.

3

Testing and Validation of Digital Systems and Technologies

Enables you to test, validate and improve digital solutions in realistic industrial environments before investment.

4

Joint Technology Solution Development

Supports the co-design, prototyping, adaptation and testing of a digital solution for a defined industrial need.

If your company has completed a valid digital maturity assessment within the last year using METU-DTX, TÜBİTAK TÜSSİDE DDX/DDX+ or SIRI, you may provide its details and apply directly for advanced services. The application remains subject to eligibility and technical assessment.

Choose your application route

Apply for the same services either under the supported DIGITALTR programme route or directly to METU DTX.

Programme-supported route

Apply under DIGITALTR

DIGITALTR is the Technical Assistance for Support for the Digital Transformation of the Manufacturing Industry and is co-financed by the European Union and the Republic of Türkiye.

For eligible applications, services under the programme may be offered at no charge or at discounted rates according to the applicant’s scale and the scope of the requested work.

  • Apply through the official portal using registration and a token link
  • Assessment against programme eligibility, capacity and portfolio balance
  • Support terms confirmed after technical assessment
Go to the DIGITALTR Application Portal
Direct service application

Apply directly to METU DTX

Apply directly to METU DTX for one or more of the four services independently of programme schedules and programme eligibility conditions.

The METU DTX team reviews your need from a technical perspective and works with you to clarify the appropriate service scope, schedule, delivery model and terms.

  • One form covering the same four service modules
  • Initial review focused on need and technical fit
  • Direct scope and schedule planning with METU DTX
