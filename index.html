From assessment to implementation

METU DTX does not position digital transformation as a stand-alone IT exercise. The operating logic links maturity assessment to business outcomes, roadmaps, project proposals and implementation gateways.

1

Assess

Capture company profile, baseline information and maturity evidence through the platform and expert-led assessment process.

2

Roadmap

Convert findings into strategic transformation areas, sequencing logic and a phased digital transformation roadmap.

3

Projectize

Translate roadmap priorities into project cards with scope, expected benefits, dependencies, timing and implementation assumptions.

4

Validate and scale

Use test-before-invest, pilot validation, laboratory infrastructure, solution-provider matching and ecosystem access before full deployment.

Independent, evidence-based and implementation-oriented.
