DIGITALTR 4th Awareness Raising Seminar

On 23 September, METU DTX will host a focused seminar on support available for the digital transformation of manufacturing, the open-call process and the essentials of preparing a strong application.

Wednesday, 23 September 2026 10:00–14:30 ETKİM Platin Hall, METU Technopolis
Register for the event Explore the seminar

METU DTX invites you to the 4th Awareness Raising Seminar organised under the Technical Assistance for Support for the Digital Transformation of the Manufacturing Industry Project (DIGITALTR), coordinated by the Ministry of Industry and Technology of the Republic of Türkiye and co-financed by the European Union and the Republic of Türkiye.

DIGITALTR aims to accelerate the digital transformation of Türkiye’s manufacturing industry, raise companies’ digital maturity and strengthen access to the technical support they need. The seminar will bring together the project team and ecosystem stakeholders to explain available services, open-call conditions and the application process.

DateWednesday, 23 September 2026
Time10:00–14:30
VenueETKİM Platin Hall, METU Technopolis
AttendanceAdvance registration is required; capacity is limited.

What will the seminar cover?

  • DIGITALTR and services available to manufacturing companies
  • Open-call services, eligibility criteria, application requirements, implementation schedule and key dates
  • Guidance and support mechanisms for preparing a strong application
  • A dedicated question-and-answer and evaluation session
  • A panel on METU DTX experience, good practices, success stories and lessons learned
  • Service stands, networking and B2B meetings
  • A guided tour of METU DTX technical infrastructure and application facilities

Who should attend?

The seminar is open to manufacturing companies, technology developers, system integrators, R&D and innovation teams, organisations seeking university-industry collaboration and other stakeholders involved in industrial digital transformation.

Capacity is limited. Registrations will be accepted in the order received. Complete the registration form before the event to secure your participation.
Information provided by project coordination: Open-call applications from companies attending the event will be handled with priority during the project’s evaluation process.

Complete your registration to explore digital transformation support, ask the project team your questions and develop new collaborations.
