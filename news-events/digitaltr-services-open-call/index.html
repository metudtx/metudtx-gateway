DIGITALTR open call

Apply for DIGITALTR digital transformation services

From assessment and roadmapping to testing, validation and joint technology development: manufacturing SMEs and mid-cap companies can apply for four METU DTX services.

1 September 2026Application period: 3 July–30 September 2026

DIGITALTR helps companies improve digital maturity, identify investment and transformation priorities, test technologies before investment and develop joint solutions for defined industrial needs.

Services open for application

  • Digital transformation maturity assessment
  • Digital transformation consultancy
  • Testing and validation of digital systems and technologies (test-before-invest)
  • Joint technology solution development

Companies may select one or several services in a single application. METU DTX supports preparation of application information, completeness and consistency checks, necessary clarification and correction steps, and routing to the appropriate service path.

Service conditions: For eligible applications, services may be offered free of charge or at discounted rates depending on the company’s scale and the scope of the requested work. Every application is assessed against programme eligibility, technical need, service capacity and expected impact. Applying does not guarantee acceptance or free support.
Companies with an existing assessment: Companies with a valid digital maturity assessment completed within the previous six months using METU DTX, TÜBİTAK TÜSSİDE DDX/DDX+ or SIRI may submit its details and apply directly for advanced services. Currency and eligibility are confirmed during technical review.

The current open-call period closes on 30 September 2026. Explore the services that fit your company and continue through the single METU DTX application route.
