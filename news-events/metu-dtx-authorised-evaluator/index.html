METU DTX becomes an authorised evaluator for the Digital Transformation Programme

The Ministry of Industry and Technology of the Republic of Türkiye has accepted the METU DTX Digital Maturity Model and added METU DTX to the authorised evaluators for its Digital Transformation Programme.

1 September 2026Ministry of Industry and Technology Digital Transformation Programme

Developed in cooperation with Fraunhofer IAO, the METU DTX Digital Maturity Model helps companies establish their current digital maturity, identify investment priorities and create an actionable transformation roadmap. Assessment reports prepared through the updated METU DTX platform in line with Ministry requirements can be used in applications to the Digital Transformation Programme.

What changes for companies?

  • Companies can complete their digital maturity assessment through METU DTX.
  • Results are reported together with investment priorities and a transformation roadmap.
  • Eligible reports that remain within the applicable six-month validity period on the application date can be used in a Ministry programme application.
  • Companies that completed a METU DTX assessment within the previous six months may contact METU DTX to confirm whether the report remains current and whether an update is required.
A current assessment is required. Earlier METU DTX reports do not automatically become valid. An assessment used for the Ministry programme must meet the applicable currency and content requirements on the application date.
Scope of the authorisation: METU DTX’s authorised-evaluator status and the preparation of an eligible assessment report do not guarantee programme acceptance, issuance of an investment incentive certificate, or approval of any incentive instrument or support amount. Final decisions remain subject to applicable rules and the Ministry’s eligibility and evaluation process.
